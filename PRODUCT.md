# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Adults (primarily 35–60) who suspect a partner of infidelity or need discreet investigation services. They arrive via paid search, emotionally distressed, seeking certainty. They need to feel safe, not judged. They're evaluating whether to trust us before they say a word.

## Product Purpose

A single-page paid ads landing page for Empire Investigation LLC — Pittsburgh's most established private investigation firm (est. 1982). The only job: convert a distressed search visitor into a consultation request or phone call. Every design decision either builds trust or destroys it.

## Brand Personality

Authoritative, Discreet, Relentless

## Anti-references

- Cheap local service sites: cluttered, coupon-heavy, amateurish regional business design
- Sensationalist true-crime: tabloid energy, dramatic crime imagery, clickbait headlines
- Generic SaaS / startup: blue gradients, Tailwind templates, san-serif-heavy consumer tech
- Law enforcement cosplay: badge graphics, police-blue palettes, detective clichés

## Design Principles

1. **Earned authority** — Every element should communicate 44 years of competence, not brag about it. Show the work, the press, the track record.
2. **Discretion as design** — Restraint is the brand. Nothing loud, garish, or exclamatory. The client is already anxious; the design should lower their heart rate.
3. **Single-minded conversion** — Every section leads to the consultation form or the phone number. No distractions, no dead ends.
4. **Dark luxury, not dark web** — The dark palette signals gravitas and premium quality, not menace. Gold is the warmth, serif is the trust.
5. **Specificity over claims** — 44 years, A+ BBB, 300+ media appearances, City's Best 3 years running. Real facts beat adjectives.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Emotionally distressed users may be on mobile, in a hurry, or using assistive technology. All text must clear 4.5:1 contrast. No motion that could increase anxiety.
